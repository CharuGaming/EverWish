# EverWish Celebration Templates Catalog

Welcome to the **EverWish** interactive template catalog. This document outlines the **10 unique templates** (5 Romantic/Valentine and 5 Birthday templates) available in the system. It describes their layout structure, key interactive components, visual style, and how they function.

---

## 💖 Romantic & Valentine's Templates

### 1. Classic Polaroid (`polaroid`)
* **Visual Theme:** Warm, nostalgic, soft pink/rose pastel scrapbook aesthetic.
* **Flow & Lockscreen:**
  * Starts with a **Lockscreen overlay** requiring the visitor to type a passcode or repeatedly tap to unlock.
  * Once unlocked, triggers a **Cursor Trail** (hearts following the mouse pointer) and plays custom background music.
* **Layout Sections:**
  1. **Interactive Hero:** Features floating, scattered Polaroid cards containing user memories.
  2. **Virtual Gift Box:** An interactive gift wrap box. When tapped, it opens with a springy animation to reveal a customized bouquet image and card message.
  3. **Love Map:** An interactive mapping component showing key milestones (e.g., "Where we first met") with customizable markers.
  4. **Memory Gallery:** A clean Polaroid grid of memories.
  5. **Things to Do (Bucket List):** A checkable list of shared dreams and future plans.
  6. **Footer:** A custom signature.

---

### 2. Modern Scroll (`modern`)
* **Visual Theme:** High-contrast, clean minimalist card design with bold typography and sleek framer-motion transitions.
* **Flow & Lockscreen:**
  * Uses the **Modern Lock Screen** with fluid gradient card overlays.
  * Transitions smoothly into a continuous, single-page scroll layout.
* **Layout Sections:**
  1. **Modern Hero:** A full-screen split-screen layout with large text overlays.
  2. **Modern Gift Box:** Minimalist glassmorphic box opening sequence.
  3. **Sleek Love Map:** High-contrast, styled map markers.
  4. **Masonry Gallery:** A dynamic, multi-column photo grid.
  5. **Footer:** Sleek footer design.

---

### 3. Premium Sweethearts (`valentine`)
* **Visual Theme:** Dreamy romance, heavy on glassmorphism (`backdrop-blur`), gold accents, and deep rose/crimson gradients.
* **Flow & Lockscreen:**
  * Features a custom theme-locked entry screen.
  * Integrates the **Global Music Player** with record-spinning animation and visible lyrics.
* **Layout Sections:**
  1. **Romantic Hero:** Elegant greeting header.
  2. **Love Letter Card:** A premium paper envelope card animation.
  3. **Custom Memory Grid:** Interactive cards that highlight and zoom on hover.
  4. **Why I Love You Jar:** An interactive mason jar containing clickable heart pills, each revealing a reason why they are loved.

---

### 4. Interactive Proposal (`proposal`)
* **Visual Theme:** Playful, high-interaction romantic layout.
* **Flow & Lockscreen:**
  * Starts with the standard Lockscreen, leading to the ultimate question.
* **Interactive Features:**
  * **The Runaway Button:** Prominently displays: *"Will you be my Valentine?"* with **Yes** and **No** buttons. 
  * If the user hovers/taps **No**, the button runs away dynamically to random positions on the screen.
  * Clicking **Yes** triggers a massive, full-screen **Heart Burst** particle effect and transitions to the main romantic letter, milestone timeline, and music player.

---

### 5. Cinematic Anniversary (`cinematic`)
* **Visual Theme:** Premium "Quiet Luxury"—deep space/night sky background (`#080612`), golden stars, and smooth cinematic animations.
* **Flow & Lockscreen:**
  * **Starfield Intro Overlay:** A full-screen starry night backdrop with a customized button (**"Tap to Open"** or custom copywriting).
  * **Video Trailer Entrance:** Clicking the button plays a full-screen, high-definition romantic intro/teaser video.
  * **Hero Video Loop:** Once the video ends or is skipped, the page transitions to a loop-video background hero section.
* **Layout Sections:**
  1. **Relationship Timer:** A real-time running clock counting down years, months, days, hours, and seconds since the relationship started.
  2. **Love Letter:** A clean card with handwritten typography and delicate drop-shadows.
  3. **Milestone Timeline:** Alternating vertical timeline entries mapping their relationship milestones with custom pictures.
  4. **Music & Lyrics Player:** Immersive audio player with scrolling text.
  5. **Why I Love You Grid:** Responsive grid cards displaying reasons.
  6. **Caught on Camera Gallery:** Masonry gallery with auto-scaling hover effects.

## 🎂 Birthday Templates

### 6. Classic Birthday (`bday1`)
* **Visual Theme:** Festive, bright, filled with floating balloons and party confetti particles.
* **Flow & Lockscreen:**
  * Bright, clean entry screen leading into standard celebration.
* **Layout Sections:**
  1. **Greeting Hero:** Bold celebration text (e.g., *"Happy Birthday, [Name]!"*).
  2. **Wishes Board:** Grid cards containing birthday messages.
  3. **Memory Gallery:** Basic image grid.
  4. **Virtual Gift:** Birthday card unboxing.

---

### 7. Stats & Wishes (`bday2`)
* **Visual Theme:** Playful infographics with vibrant, happy pastel cards.
* **Flow & Lockscreen:**
  * Quick unlock lead-in with floating decoration.
* **Layout Sections:**
  1. **Life Stats Dashboard:** An interactive calculator showing fun metrics about the celebrant since their birth date (e.g., total years, months, days, hours, and minutes lived).
  2. **Wishes Board:** Colorful polaroid-style card wishes from friends.
  3. **Scattered Memory Gallery:** Collage layout.

---

### 8. Gift Unbox (`bday3`)
* **Visual Theme:** High-interaction 3D birthday party theme.
* **Flow & Lockscreen:**
  * Entry overlay with party music cues.
* **Layout Sections:**
  1. **3D Virtual Gift Box:** The centerpiece. A large, beautifully rendered gift box that shakes when tapped, slides open its lid, and releases particle bursts to reveal the primary birthday present image/gift voucher.
  2. **Wishes Carousel:** Auto-sliding cards of birthday wishes.

---

### 9. Celebration Slide (`bday4`)
* **Visual Theme:** Dynamic slideshow/carousel focus with smooth sliding transitions.
* **Flow & Lockscreen:**
  * Quick unlock leading to slide decks.
* **Layout Sections:**
  1. **Memory Carousel Slideshow:** Slideshow viewer that scrolls through high-resolution memories.
  2. **Music Deck:** Integrated audio console with custom player.

---

### 10. Cinematic Birthday (`bday5`)
* **Visual Theme:** Premium celebration aesthetics. Velvet/dark rich backgrounds, warm gold accents, elegant handwritten typography, and seamless transitions.
* **Flow & Lockscreen:**
  * **Intro Overlay:** Features dynamic floating birthday emojis (🎂, 🎈, 🎉) with a customizable call-to-action button (editable via Admin Panel, e.g., *"Open Surprise"*).
  * **Intro Video Teaser:** Tapping the button plays a full-screen birthday teaser/trailer video.
  * **Loop Video BG Hero:** The main hero page transitions into a warm loop-video background.
* **Layout Sections:**
  1. **Polaroid Grid:** A 6-photo masonry grid that preserves aspect ratios (no cropping). Tapping any photo triggers a **"Locus Unwrap"** animation where the photo smoothly scales up into a full-screen lightbox.
  2. **Love Letter:** A handwriting-styled scroll-reveal letter on elegant premium paper.
  3. **Interactive Gift Box:** Tap-to-open 3D gift box that unfolds to reveal a custom image and message.
  4. **Birthday Bucket List:** Checkable milestones for the celebrant's upcoming year.
